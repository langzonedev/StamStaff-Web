# UX Principles

- Calm and obvious: one primary next action and plain-language status.
- Honest: a provisional claim is never labelled as a confirmed shift.
- Forgiving: preserve input/context through recoverable failures and make release/correction easy.
- Inclusive: usable by people with varied age, vision, dexterity, and technical confidence.
- Responsive: phone-first without degrading tablet or desktop use.
- Accessible: target WCAG 2.2 AA with semantic controls, keyboard/focus support, labels, contrast, zoom/reflow, reduced motion, and touch targets of at least 44 by 44 CSS pixels.
- State-complete: design loading, empty, pending, open, full, conflict, offline, success, failure, confirmed, and locked states.
- More than colour: unavailable/full capacity includes text and programmatic semantics, not grey styling alone.
- Fictional and truthful: sample data is synthetic; demo limitations remain visible inside the product.

Customer-specific branding must not be added until approved assets and usage authority are documented privately. Other Lang Systems product identities, terminology, and sample workflows must not be copied.
